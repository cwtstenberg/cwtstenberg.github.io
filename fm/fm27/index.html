Fit finder

Tick a player's best attributes (roughly 14+). Roles are scored 2 points per matching priority attribute and 1 per supporting attribute, shown as a share of that role's maximum. Use the position and phase filters above to narrow it down.

Role list follows the FM26/FM27 IP/OOP system as read from the installed game by the community. Priority/supporting attributes are compiled from community guides (mainly TouchlineFM's FM27 role pages) and are a guide, not SI's official numbers. In-game, the attributes highlighted on a player's profile when you select a role are the final word, so check there before a big signing.
