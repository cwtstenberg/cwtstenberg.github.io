How the score is calculated

Key role attributes use base weight 3.0. Preferred role attributes use 1.5. Other attributes do not contribute to that role.

Match-engine modifiers: Acceleration ×1.35, Pace ×1.35, Work Rate ×1.20, Agility ×1.15, Jumping Reach ×1.15, Anticipation ×1.10, Balance ×1.10, Stamina ×1.05 and Strength ×1.05.

Rating = weighted attribute average ÷ 20 × 100.

Important: positional familiarity is not included, so this is an attribute-fit calculator rather than a full in-game role suitability grade.
